Swachhata Hi Seva 2026

Campaign period: 17 September–2 October 2026 · Regional Labour Institute, Kolkata

Official campaign portal ↗

Clean premises and responsible disposal

Cleanliness, space optimisation and waste management

Intensive cleaning covered approximately 10,000 sq. ft. of premises. Scrap and obsolete condemned items were segregated for disposal, generating more than ₹50,000 in revenue. Activities also included cleaning against mosquito breeding and awareness on avoiding single-use plastics.

Participation and community action

Jan Bhagidari and workplace ownership

38 Cleanliness Target Units (CTUs) were completed, alongside intensive cleaning at 4 additional public places. Officers, staff, students and programme participants contributed more than 350 man-hours. Activities included the Swachhata pledge, “Ek Din Ek Ghanta Ek Saath”, “Ek Ped Maa Ke Naam”, and a Safai Mitra health check-up and facilitation programme with the local municipality.
